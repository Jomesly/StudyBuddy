Privacy & monitoring

The original Python engine runs on this computer. It uses your original YOLOv8 model, MediaPipe face/head/mouth/hand detection, foreground window titles, and alert sound files. Camera images and app titles stay on this computer; preview images travel only over the local connection. No video recording or microphone is used. Pause or End session stops monitoring.

Full monitoring continues when you switch tabs or applications, until the session ends. It identifies active browser tabs from their visible window title, as the original app did; it does not read browsing history or URLs. Browser-only mode has limited tab visibility checks and suspends camera detection when hidden. Completed sessions and notes are saved in this browser; export or clear them in History.
